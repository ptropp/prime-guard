Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information PRIME may process and how that information is used.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Overview

PRIME respects the privacy of Discord users and communities. This Privacy Policy describes the information that may be processed when PRIME is installed or used in a Discord server.

2. Information PRIME May Process

Depending on the features enabled and Discord permissions granted, PRIME may process information necessary to operate its security, moderation, and server management features.

This may include:

  • Discord server IDs.
  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord channel IDs.
  • Discord role IDs.
  • Configuration and moderation settings.
  • Server activity and event information required for PRIME's security features.
  • Information provided through commands or interactions with PRIME.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by PRIME may be used to:

  • Provide PRIME's security and moderation features.
  • Respond to commands and interactions.
  • Maintain server-specific configuration.
  • Detect and respond to potential security concerns.
  • Improve reliability and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Protect PRIME and Discord communities from abuse.

4. Message Content

PRIME does not sell or publicly distribute Discord message content.

If a PRIME feature requires access to message content for moderation, security, or command functionality, that information is only processed as necessary to provide the relevant feature and protect the Discord community.

5. Data Sharing

PRIME does not sell personal information.

Information processed by PRIME is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable legal obligations, protect the security of PRIME or its users, or when otherwise required by law.

6. Data Retention

PRIME retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its services, maintain server functionality, resolve technical or security issues, or comply with applicable obligations.

Some configuration or operational data may be deleted when PRIME is removed from a Discord server, subject to reasonable backup and technical retention periods.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users or server owners may request information about relevant data processed by PRIME or request deletion of applicable stored data.

Data requests can be made through the official PRIME support channels.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to help protect information processed by PRIME. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

PRIME is intended for use through Discord in accordance with Discord's applicable Terms of Service and minimum age requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as PRIME develops or its features change. Updated versions will be published on this website.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or requests relating to data can be directed to the official PRIME support channels.

PRIME is committed to using Discord-related information only as reasonably necessary to provide and maintain its services.
